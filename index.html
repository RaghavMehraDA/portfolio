AI Automation

Human → AI → Automation → Result

AI automation means letting a model handle the repetitive decision or writing step inside a process, while the surrounding system keeps things reliable. The flow below shows how those pieces connect.

  1. 01

    User

    A request, a form submission or a message enters the system.

  2. 02

    AI

    A model interprets the input, summarises it or produces a draft.

  3. 03

    Automation

    Rules decide what happens next — route, store, notify or escalate.

  4. 04

    Process

    The connected systems carry the work out, step by step.

  5. 05

    Result

    A finished output, a saved record or a human review point.
